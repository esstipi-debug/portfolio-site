Case Study — AI Agent

Building an AI Agent for Supply-Chain Decisions

Why I didn't stop at spreadsheets: a QA-gated agent that classifies a brief, runs the right model, and never leaves you with a dead end.

Problem

Most inventory analysis lives in one-off spreadsheets: fragile, unauditable, and slow to rerun the moment the data changes. Every new question means rebuilding the same formulas from scratch, and nothing stops a bad input from producing a confident-looking wrong answer.

Method

I built an orchestrator around the exact same pipeline for every capability: a plain-language brief is matched to one of 50 registered tools, run, validated against a QA gate, and delivered as a finished Excel workbook, report, and chart. If QA fails, nothing ships — that check lives in one place, not scattered across each tool. The matching is by keyword first; a language model is consulted only to break a tie, and its answer is discarded unless it names a registered tool. Every number in every deliverable comes from deterministic code, and with no model available at all the pipeline still runs.

What I built

Two safety guarantees sit on top of the pipeline. Never-unprotected: every consequential result is either executed outright, or hands back ranked options, a prepared handoff (a pre-filled PO or count sheet), or an escalation with an SLA — never a dead end. Safe-staging writeback: any change to a system of record (e.g. an Odoo ERP or an Excel workbook) is first staged as a dry-run changeset, classified by risk tier, gated behind a time-boxed approval, and applied idempotently with a working rollback. The write paths are built and tested against in-memory stand-ins; the deployed app is read-only by design, and they have not been run against a client’s live system.
